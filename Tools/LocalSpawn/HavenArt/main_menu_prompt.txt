Wide cinematic key art for the title screen of a dark Nordic folklore survival game. Dusk in a small hidden village in a forest clearing: timber longhouses and cabins with turf roofs and carved gables, warm glowing windows, a wooden fence, stacked firewood. On the right third of the picture stands an ancient portal: a tall arch of rune-carved standing stones, its runes and the gate inside it glowing with soft golden-white light that spills across the grass. In front of the houses an open stone hearth burns with a lively crackling fire, sparks drifting up, warm amber light on the ground and the walls. The left part of the picture is calmer and darker, open grass in soft shadow, leaving room for a menu. Behind the village rises a dense dark pine forest on the slopes, thick mist drifting between the trunks, cold blue-green moonlight. Deep in the misty forest, half hidden between the trees, huge shadowy creatures lurk: the silhouette of a towering troll, a hunched beast, pairs of faintly glowing eyes, barely visible and threatening. Strong contrast between the warm, safe, firelit village in front and the cold, dangerous misty forest behind. Clear depth layers: foreground grass and stones, the village and the glowing portal in the middle, the forest and mist behind, far mountains under a dusky sky. Hand-painted, painterly concept art, rich atmosphere, no people in the foreground, no text, no logo, no modern objects.
