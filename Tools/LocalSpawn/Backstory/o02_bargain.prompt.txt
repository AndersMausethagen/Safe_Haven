Wide view of a sunlit summer forest clearing ringed by enormous ancient pines. In the centre stands an ancient arch of tall cracked standing stones carved with runes, the runes glowing faintly gold. On the left side of the stones stand grown men and women of a village, adults in long wool cloaks, seen from behind. On the right side, at the tree line, wait three towering guardians made of roots, bark and moss, each twice as tall as a man, heads bowed, still as trees. Behind the guardians the forest goes deep and dark and cannot be made out. Shafts of golden sunlight fall onto the stones between the two sides. Dark tree trunks and ferns frame the left and right edges in the near foreground.
