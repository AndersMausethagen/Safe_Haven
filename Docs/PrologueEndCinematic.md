# Prologue-end cinematic: "The Catch"

**Status:** built, 2026-11-10: it plays as the prologue ends (1:52). Seen in screenshots of every shot and run for real
from a new game on throwaway saves (the prologue ended, the cinematic played over it, home loaded behind it); nobody has
listened to it yet. What differs from this script is in section 6.

A painted, narrated bridge between the prologue and home, in the manner of the opening ([BackstoryCinematic.md](BackstoryCinematic.md)):
nine shots, each a still painting cut into layers and moved by a slow camera. It plays when the countdown at the
waystones runs out (or the player falls in that last defence), after "THE CONNECTION BREAKS" and before home loads.

It answers the three things the player cannot know at that moment:

1. **What happened** when they put their hand on the stone: the way opened, the old troll's blow landed, the stones
   broke and the road tore, and everyone in the ring was thrown.
2. **Who saved them:** the portal, asleep a hundred winters, felt the hand, reached, and caught one. Only one.
3. **Why they are here and what to do:** the others are alive but the forest forgets the lost; a tended hearth is the
   one thing it cannot forget. Light a fire, build a home, wake the door, go and bring them back.

## 1. The frame

| | |
|---|---|
| **Plays** | At the end of the prologue, both endings (held out, or overwhelmed). Home loads behind it. Hold a key to skip. |
| **Narrator** | The portal, to the player ("little one"), in the voice of the opening and of its lines at home. |
| **Length** | About 1:40 (175 spoken words at the portal's pace). Real shot lengths come from the recorded lines. |
| **Ends on** | The player asleep at the foot of the portal's stones at dawn, which is where they wake. |

### What it leaves out on purpose

- **Why the old troll came.** That it came for the door is Ingrid's to tell when she is found, and the portal's at the
  end of Act I. Here it is only "already walking towards the stones".
- **Who let the hearth go cold**, and what the guardians became: Act I's reveals, as in the opening.
- **Why the stones woke for the player's hand** when they would not for Sigrid's. The portal doesn't know.
- **The names of the seven.** The portal's lines at home and its Haven page name them.

### What it repeats

The portal's first conversation at home says some of this again ("I caught you. I could only catch one.", the seven
sparks, "So light one"). That conversation is where the player answers and is given the first quest, so it stays; with
this in front of it, it can be trimmed if it reads as twice-told in play.

## 2. The shots

| # | Shot | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hand on the stone | "You put your hand on the stone, little one. And far away, in my sleep, I felt it." |
| 2 | The old one | "The old one felt it too. It was already walking towards the stones." |
| 3 | The road tears | "Its blow fell as the way opened. The stones broke, and the road tore." |
| 4 | Scattered | "Everyone in that ring was thrown into the dark. Seven of your people are out there now, scattered like sparks across the Misty Woods." |
| 5 | The catch | "I am old, and thin, and I had slept a hundred winters. But I reached. I caught one. I could only catch one." |
| 6 | The clearing | "So here you are: my clearing. The last place the forest has not taken." |
| 7 | The forgetting | "The others are alive. But the forest does not kill the lost. It forgets them. And it has already begun." |
| 8 | A hearth | "There is one thing it cannot forget: a hearth that someone tends. Light one beside my stones, and they will see it, and know which way is home." |
| 9 | Wake up | "Then build them something to come home to. Wake me, and I will open the way, and you will go and bring them back. Now wake up, little one." |

### 1. The hand on the stone

- **Picture:** close, at night in a storm. A hand pressed flat on a tall weathered standing stone; the runes under and
  around the palm lit gold. Rain. Everything else blue-black.
- **Camera:** pushes in slowly on the hand.
- **Moving details:** rain; the rune light swelling from the palm outwards as the line is said.
- **Voice:** quiet, close. A pause after "little one".

### 2. The old one

- **Picture:** the ring of standing stones in a stormy forest clearing, small figures with torches inside it. Above the
  treetops behind them, the huge dark shape of a mountain troll.
- **Camera:** rises from the ring towards the shape.
- **Moving details:** rain, torch flicker, a slow flash of lightning behind the troll.
- **Voice:** grave, even.

### 3. The road tears

- **Picture:** the ring from within as it breaks: a burst of white-gold light at the centre, stones cracking and
  leaning, shards and roots flung outwards, small figures thrown as dark shapes against the light.
- **Camera:** a short, fast push in that eases to a stop.
- **Moving details:** the light flares on "broke" and dies to nothing on "tore"; sparks fly outward.
- **Voice:** plain, not loud. The picture carries the violence.

### 4. Scattered

- **Picture:** from high above, an endless dark forest under mist, at night.
- **Camera:** a long pull back.
- **Moving details:** seven small warm lights fly outward from the centre and come to rest far apart, then dim.
- **Voice:** slower. "Seven of your people" counted, not hurried.

### 5. The catch

- **Picture:** a small hooded figure falling through the dark among grasping roots; below and behind it, a tall arch
  of warm gold light reaching up like open hands.
- **Camera:** falls with the figure, then stops as the light takes it.
- **Moving details:** the gold light swells on "I reached" and holds; motes rise through it.
- **Voice:** tired, then certain. "I could only catch one" quieter, with regret.

### 6. The clearing

- **Picture:** dawn. A small green clearing ringed by enormous dark trees, the arch of cracked standing stones in it, a
  stream, and a hooded figure asleep in the grass at the foot of the stones.
- **Camera:** pulls back to show how small the clearing is.
- **Moving details:** fireflies, soft mist at the tree line.
- **Voice:** gentler. The first warmth since the opening.

### 7. The forgetting

- **Picture:** deep in the misty forest. One small figure with a dim lantern stands lost between huge trunks, far off.
- **Camera:** tracks slowly sideways; near trunks slide across and hide the figure.
- **Moving details:** mist closes in; the lantern dims on "It has already begun".
- **Voice:** "It forgets them" quieter than the sentence before, as in the opening.

### 8. A hearth

- **Picture:** dusk in the clearing. A small campfire burns beside the standing stones; its light reaches the tree
  line and the mist stands back from it.
- **Camera:** pushes in towards the fire.
- **Moving details:** flames, sparks, the firelight breathing; on "they will see it" a far light answers once in the
  dark of the trees.
- **Voice:** warm, sure. This is the instruction: say it plainly.

### 9. Wake up

- **Picture:** the clearing of shot 6 again, closer on the sleeping figure and the stones.
- **Camera:** pushes in on the figure.
- **Moving details:** the runes brighten slowly; morning light grows; fade to white on "wake up".
- **Voice:** near a smile on the last line.

## 3. Sound and music

- **Music:** *Misty woods - Eerie raid*, low, under the storm shots (1 to 5); *Safe haven - Nightsong* from the dawn
  clearing (6) to the end, as the opening uses them.
- **Sounds:** wind under the cold shots; the game's own fire, birds and creaks where the pictures have them.
- **Mix:** the voice leads; music and effects duck under each line.

## 6. As built

- **Length:** 1:52 (82 s of speech, 21 sentences, each its own clip). At the pace of the portal's dialogue the short
  sentences came to over two minutes, so the narration is a little quicker than her lines at home (135 words a minute).
- **Voice:** `portal_f_british_northern` through LocalSpawn's acting voice model, calm throughout, with sorrow on "I
  could only catch one" and "It forgets them" and warmth from the clearing on. The opening was spoken by the other
  voice model (Qwen3-TTS) in the same voice: listen whether the two sound like one narrator.
- **Shot 3:** the four are running from the light, not thrown.
- **Shot 4:** seen from a hill across the forest under a moon, not from straight above. The seven sparks are lights
  added in the cinematic: two near, five small towards the horizon.
- **Shot 5:** the light is a tall opening in a great tree, not an arch of stones; dark hands reach from the left edge.
- **Shot 6 and 9:** painted without LocalSpawn's Home style, whose own words ask for timber cabins: with it the
  clearing came with one cabin, and with three when told to have none. The first arch also carried near-letters.
- **Shot 8:** four standing stones around the fire and no arch; a cabin stands at the left edge, which suits "build
  them something to come home to".
- **The end** fades to black as the opening does (the loading screen that follows is dark); the morning light grows
  first.
- **Sounds:** rain under shots 1 to 3, the old troll's footfalls in 2, stone breaking and the ward falling in 3, wind
  under 4, 5 and 7, the ward mending on "I reached", birds in 6 and 9. No new sounds were made for it.
- **Measured in the Editor:** home loading in the background held one frame for 0.36 s near the start (the cinematic
  waits); after a skip at 8 s, home was ready 4.7 s later.
