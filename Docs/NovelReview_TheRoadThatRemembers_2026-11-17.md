# Review: *The Road That Remembers*

Reviewed 2026-11-17 from a copy of `Docs/The Road That Remembers.docx` (about 14,700 words: Prologue, 31 chapters, Epilogue). The whole book was read. The manuscript was not changed.

Locations are given as chapter plus a short quote, so you can find each one with Word's search. Where a finding depends on how a passage is meant to be read, it says so.

---

## Summary

**What works.** The prose is plain, clear and mostly free of ornament, which suits the book. The central idea (names, memory and fire as the things that hold people together) is strong and runs all the way through. The dialogue is dry and often funny ("That's not a distance." / "It is in these woods."). The chapter endings pull you on. Several scenes are very good: the knuckle-scar test on Mara's mother (Ch 17), Bread choosing his name (Ch 16–17), the Waker's "old bear" test at the pool (Ch 27), and Sigrun's return (Ch 31). The repeated lines ("You kept their fire", "Hearth-keeper") pay off well.

**The biggest problems:**

1. **Who is lost, who is found, and who is present does not add up.** Runa and Eirik come back without ever being found. Sigrid and Liv are at the campfire in Chapters 15–18 but are rescued in Chapter 26. Sigrun is at the First Hearth in Chapter 29 but only arrives "three days later" in Chapter 31. Chapter 23 says the scouts went ahead, yet Tor, Sigrid and Bjarne are with the group in Chapters 24 and 26. The unnamed traveler from Chapter 1 disappears. Bjarne is a man, then a woman, then a man again. This is the most important thing to fix (see the cast tracker below).
2. **Objects jump around.** The silver button is lost and then shown to Asa. The coal is placed in a stone and then found in Mara's coat. In Chapter 30 the Waker runs off holding the ember, yet Mara puts it in the basin. The red thread appears five chapters before it is found.
3. **Rules are introduced and then dropped.** The Life Orbs, where the three bound stones are, Ingrid's blue-flame vial, the bell, the mountain troll, and the route through the blue door.
4. **Some terms are used before they are introduced:** "the Waker" (Ch 15), "the Hunger" (Ch 15), "the Portal" (Ch 9), and the Portal stones the party carries in Act II. Game words also leak into the prose: "raid road", "extraction light", "Life Orbs".
5. **The second half of Act I is mostly summary, and some of it repeats.** Chapter 18 goes over Chapters 14–15 again, while the rescues readers care most about (Sigrid, Liv, Runa, Eirik) are never shown.

## 1. Inconsistencies

Most serious first.

**I1. Runa and Eirik return without being found** (Ch 12)
- *Where:* Ch 12: "Ingrid wrote the names. Runa repeated them aloud. Eirik checked the spelling."
- *Problem:* Both were scattered when the troll broke the ring (Ch 6). No chapter shows them being found.
- *Why it matters:* Readers have followed these two since Chapter 1. Their return is a payoff the book skips, and readers will think they missed a page.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 12 is already a montage of returns, so add two short paragraphs in the same style. For example: "Runa was found asleep in a hunter's blind, her bowstring wound around her wrist so she could not drop it. Eirik walked out of the mist on his own, repeating his sister's name under his breath." (Keep Sigrid and Liv separate; see I2.)

**I2. Sigrid and Liv are home in Act I but rescued in Act III** (Ch 15–18 against Ch 23 and 26)
- *Where:* Ch 15: "Ingrid, Bjarne, Tor, Liv. Sigrid. Eirik. Runa" (read out as people found). Ch 16: "Sigrid took the watch." Ch 17: "Sigrid understood first." Ch 18: "the four scouts, Sigrid, Eirik, Runa…". Then Ch 26: "They found Sigrid beneath a fallen trunk. She had one arm around Liv…"
- *Problem:* Either they were found in Act I (the text suggests this, but never shows it) or they are lost until Chapter 26. The book says both.
- *Why it matters:* Sigrid leads the party and Liv narrates the prologue. Their status is one of the book's main threads.
- *Suggest (recommended):* Rescue them in Act I. Give them a short scene, because Liv opened the book and deserves one. Then treat Chapter 26 as a *new* separation inside the valley (see I3). That keeps the Chapter 16–18 scenes and the Chapter 26 rescue as they are.

**I3. "The four scouts had gone ahead" contradicts the next three chapters** (Ch 23, 24, 26)
- *Where:* Ch 23: "The four scouts had gone ahead to find the road. Liv returned once with a warning, then went back with Sigrid when the valley shifted; Tor and Bjarne vanished on the far side of the fold, and Gunnar followed their signal." Then in Ch 24: "Tor used scraps of red cloth" and "At last Sigrid ordered everyone to walk in pairs". In Ch 26: "The four scouts had gone ahead… Sigrid kept Mara and the others on the main path", while Bjarne is in the main group ("'And what is that?' Bjarne asked").
- *Problem:* The same separation is told twice (Ch 23 and Ch 26). Characters who have left are still present, and Sigrid is in two places in the same chapter.
- *Why it matters:* This sentence carries a lot of offstage action. Because it contradicts the scenes after it, readers lose track of who is where during the climax.
- *Suggest:* Delete the Chapter 23 sentence. Rewrite the opening of Chapter 26 as: "Liv, Tor and Gunnar had gone ahead to mark a route around the ravine. When the valley shifted, Sigrid went after them. Ingrid kept the rest of them on the main path, but the folded forest stretched between them before they had walked a hundred paces." Bjarne then stays with the group, and finding Tor and Gunnar, then Sigrid and Liv, works as written.

**I4. Sigrun is at the First Hearth before she comes home** (Ch 29 against Ch 31)
- *Where:* Ch 29: "Eirik and Sigrun stood shoulder to shoulder, red thread circling their joined wrists." Ch 31: "Sigrun came through the stones three days later…"
- *Problem:* In Chapter 21 she was only seen through a window ("Somewhere"). She is never found before Chapter 29.
- *Why it matters:* Chapter 31's reunion is one of the best scenes. Chapter 29 spoils it and makes it impossible.
- *Suggest:* Change the Chapter 29 line to: "Eirik stood alone, the red thread tied around his wrist."

**I5. Bjarne's gender changes** (Prologue, Ch 10, Ch 13)
- *Where:* Prologue: "Bjarne was tightening the strap on his shield" and "The three men…". Ch 10: "A woman in expedition mail… a smith's hammer at her belt". Ch 13: "Bjarne struck one with his hammer."
- *Why it matters:* Readers will notice, and it makes them doubt other details.
- *Suggest:* Pick one. Bjarne is normally a man's name. If you want a woman, rename her (for example Bera) and fix the prologue and Chapter 13. If you want a man, fix Chapter 10 ("A man in travel-worn mail… a smith's hammer at his belt").

**I6. The prologue's "three men" leaves out Ingrid** (Prologue)
- *Where:* "The three men glanced at the trees." "The three men heard it."
- *Problem:* Chapter 1 says the four scouts were Ingrid, Bjarne, Tor and Liv. Ingrid is a woman (Ch 8: "the woman asked") and is never shown in the prologue.
- *Suggest:* Use "The other three…" both times, and give Ingrid one small action. Holding the lantern would suit her: "Ingrid held the lantern closer."

**I7. Runa's introduction is given to the wrong speaker** (Ch 1)
- *Where:* "A stocky man beside Sigrid laughed into his beard. 'That's Eirik. I'm Runa.'"
- *Problem:* As written, the bearded man is speaking, so he is Runa. But Runa is a woman (Ch 2: "her bow").
- *Why it matters:* It confuses the main cast on the first page they appear.
- *Suggest:* "A stocky man beside Sigrid laughed into his beard. The woman next to him, a bow across her back, nodded at him. 'That's Eirik. I'm Runa. We're the people you'll have to keep alive while you ask them.'"

**I8. The silver button is lost, then shown to Asa** (Ch 4, 7, 12)
- *Where:* Ch 4: "Eirik turned the child's button… He had taken it from Mara without asking." Ch 7: "The notebooks were gone. So was the village button." Ch 12: "When Mara showed her the button, Asa touched it…"
- *Suggest:* The simplest fix is in Chapter 12: "When Mara described the button, Asa held out her own." Alternatively, cut "So was the village button" in Chapter 7 and have Eirik hand it back in Chapter 4.

**I9. The coal and ember do not stay continuous** (Ch 6, 7, 14, 28, 29)
- *Where:* Ch 6: Mara "placed it in the groove at the stone's base." Ch 7: "She found a single charred coal in the fold of her coat." Ch 14: she "drove the burning coal into the knot at its center." Ch 28: "Mara took the ember from her lantern. It had followed her since the first night." Ch 29: "Mara took the ember from the lantern" a second time.
- *Problem:* The coal is used up twice (in the stone, then in the Heartroot) and then said to have been with her all along. It is also taken out of the lantern twice.
- *Why it matters:* The ember is the book's central object, so readers track it.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 7: "…a coal she must have caught when the stone flared." Chapter 28: "Its fire had followed her since the first night: lit from the clearing's fire, which had been lit from that one coal." Delete one of the two "took the ember from the lantern" lines. Keep the Chapter 29 one and in Chapter 28 write "Mara looked at the ember in her lantern."

**I10. The red thread appears before it is found** (Ch 16)
- *Where:* Ch 16: "Eirik sat with his back to the fire, holding the red thread from the snow valley."
- *Problem:* The snow valley and the thread come in Chapter 21.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 21 says Sigrun had already stitched a red thread into his coat, so use that: "…running his thumb over the red thread his sister had stitched into his coat." This also plants the thread early.

**I11. Eirik's weapon changes from spear to sword** (Ch 1–14 against Ch 19, 21, 30)
- *Where:* "a short spear slung over his shoulder" (Ch 1) and "Eirik planted his spear" (Ch 14), then "Eirik put a hand to his sword" (Ch 19), "Eirik's hand fell away from his sword" (Ch 21), "Eirik's sword came free" (Ch 30).
- *Suggest:* Use the spear throughout, or show him picking up a sword (Bjarne could forge him one in Chapter 11).

**I12. The three bound stones are in two places** (Ch 7, 9, 10, 12, 13)
- *Where:* Ch 7: "Beyond the clearing, three root-choked stones answered." Ch 10: "the three bound stones glimmered in the distance." But in Ch 12 "Hild knelt beside the three bound stones", and in Ch 13 "every person in the clearing" hears the first stone wake. Ch 9 also has Ingrid reading three runes on the *arch* ("The first stone is for a flame…").
- *Why it matters:* Readers cannot picture the clearing, and it is unclear whether the three runes are on the arch or on three separate stones.
- *Suggest:* Put the stones at the clearing's edge in all chapters. Chapter 7: "At the clearing's edge, three root-choked stones…". Chapter 10: "…glimmered across the clearing." In Chapter 9, add: "The same three marks were cut into the bound stones at the edge of the grass."

**I13. The Life Orbs are set up and then forgotten** (Ch 9, 10, 13)
- *Where:* Ch 9: "Mara would need to find the Life Orbs that held them, then carry each home." Ch 10: she finds two orbs, then "Mara had room to take one thing. She chose Bjarne." Ch 13: the stones are freed by cutting roots, and the orbs are never mentioned again.
- *Problem:* It is unclear whether she brought the orbs home, and "room to take one thing" comes after she has already picked up two.
- *Suggest:* Either cut the orbs (the Portal could say: "Something in the woods feeds the roots that hold them. Find it and cut it.") or use them. Chapter 10: "She could carry the last orb or Bjarne, not both. She chose Bjarne." Chapter 13: "When Mara set an orb at its foot, the roots loosened enough to cut."

**I14. Gunnar's past changes** (Ch 12 against Ch 26)
- *Where:* Ch 12: found at a lake, "a fishing spear and a map of stars… entered the woods in winter. His beard had not grown since." Ch 26: "Gunnar had arrived in the valley as an old man and stepped out of it as a young one… He could name the battle where he had been wounded, but not the town he had defended."
- *Problem:* He goes from fisherman to soldier. He also "arrived in the valley" (Troll Valley) only in Chapter 23, so "arrived as an old man" reads as if it happened offstage just now.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 26: "The valley had taken years off Gunnar, and things with them. He could name the lake where he had fished, but not the village he had fished for."

**I15. The Waker's first door is in two places** (Ch 24 against Ch 29)
- *Where:* Ch 24, at an arch in the valley: "I opened this door once… I left a village behind me." Ch 29: "He followed them to the old stones… opened it with a stolen ember."
- *Suggest:* Chapter 24: "I opened a door like this once." The button can stay as a sign that he passed through the valley searching.

**I16. The route to the First Hearth changes** (Ch 25 against Ch 27)
- *Where:* Ch 25: "a blue door beneath the road. The route to the First Hearth lay beyond it, but the Portal could not open it without the Waker's consent. He gave that consent." Ch 27: "The route to the First Hearth began at a pool beneath the mountain." The blue door is never used.
- *Suggest:* Begin Chapter 27 with: "Beyond the blue door, the route to the First Hearth began at a pool beneath the mountain." (Also give a reason for the consent; see C15.)

**I17. Bread is both found and missing** (Ch 18, 30, 31)
- *Where:* Ch 18 lists him at the fire. Ch 30: "The lost soldier whose name none of them knew". Ch 31: "the names of the missing… The soldier with no name."
- *Suggest:* Decide where he is during Acts II–III. A good option is that he stays behind to keep the clearing fire, which also answers who tends it (C6). Chapter 30: "Bread, who still did not know the name he was born with." In Chapter 31, take him off the missing list, or write "the name Bread was born with" instead.

**I18. The Portal says it doesn't remember, then admits it kept quiet** (Ch 12–13 against Ch 14 and 18)
- *Where:* Ch 12: "I do not remember." Ch 13: "I have been asleep a long time." Ch 14: "I did not know how to tell the truth without making you hate the forest." Ch 18: "I had forgotten the words for blame."
- *Problem:* Withholding the truth and not remembering it are different things. Mara, who checks everything, does not notice.
- *Suggest:* Keep one of the "Why didn't you tell us?" exchanges (see O1) and let Mara notice. For example: "You said you didn't remember." / "I remembered this."

**I19. "It had not attacked them"** (Ch 18 against Ch 14)
- *Where:* Ch 14: "Then it struck." Ch 18: "It had not attacked them."
- *Suggest:* "It had not meant to attack them."

**I20. Who left home, Eirik or his sister?** (Ch 3 against Ch 21 and 31)
- *Where:* Ch 3: "When my sister left home…". Ch 21: "She stitched a red thread into my coat before I left." Ch 31: "older than the boy she remembered."
- *Suggest:* Chapter 3: "When I left home, I knew the sound of her steps on the stair. This morning I had to think to remember her name."

**I21. "Troll toll"** (Ch 21)
- *Where:* "The troll toll had taken the sound of his sister's laugh."
- *Problem:* The toll was taken by the antlered stone guardian at the Wolf's Jaw, not the mountain troll.
- *Suggest:* "The guardian's toll had taken…"

**I22. The Heartroot branch is both rooted and carried** (Ch 15, 19, 31)
- *Where:* Ch 15: "The Heartroot branch took root beside the campfire." Ch 19: "They left the Heartroot at dawn, carrying a branch wrapped in damp cloth." Ch 31: "Beside it, the Heartroot branch had taken root" at the meadow stone ring.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 19: "They left the clearing at dawn, carrying a cutting from the Heartroot branch wrapped in damp cloth…"

**Smaller inconsistencies**
- Ch 2, "a wooden box that had not been there when they left the ridge": the party set out from the meadow. Only Mara crossed the ridge. Use "…when they loaded the cart."
- Ch 2, "a village Mara knew had burned down decades ago", against Ch 12, "a village that had not existed for generations". Use the same span of time in both.
- Ch 3 and Ch 5, "The four travelers": five people are present (Sigrid, Eirik, Runa, the traveler and Mara). Use "The five of them".
- Ch 20, "He had a healer's careful hands", against Ch 29, "repairing a mill wheel". Use "a craftsman's careful hands".
- Ch 22, Yrsa "had lived through a winter that had not happened yet", against Ch 12, where she arrives *late* ("Then I'm late"). *Interpretation:* if she comes from the future, Chapter 12 should hint at it. If she comes from the past, change Chapter 22.

## 2. Things that don't make sense

**S1. The Waker runs off with the ember, yet Mara lights the basin with it** (Ch 30)
- *Where:* "He seized it and ran for the open door… He did not let go of the ember." Later: "Mara set the ember among them." Then: "The Waker sat on the stone floor, empty-handed."
- *Why it matters:* This is the climax, and the key object is in two hands at once.
- *Suggest:* Mara is already holding the ember at the end of Chapter 29, so have him grab the lantern (with its small blue flame) instead. "The Waker moved before Mara could lower the ember. He seized the lantern, with what was left of its flame, and ran for the open door." Later: "He did not let go of the lantern." Then "Mara ran to the basin. The ember in her hand was not enough." The ending ("empty-handed") still works, because the lantern is lost through the door.

**S2. Sigrid asks "Why?" about something she can't know was left out** (Ch 4)
- *Where:* "She left out the part about its warmth. 'Why?' Sigrid asked."
- *Suggest:* "She left out the part about its warmth. Sigrid noticed the pause. 'What did you skip?' 'The mitten was warm.' 'Why leave it out?' 'Because I can't prove it.'"

**S3. The cave is sealed by roots, yet they walk out and see the trail** (Ch 5)
- *Where:* "Eirik… dragged the bodies outside… Sigrid looked at the trail north… Sigrid glanced behind them. The cave mouth opened onto a wall of roots."
- *Suggest:* Make the *road back* sealed, not the cave: "Sigrid glanced back the way they had come. Roots stood across the road in a wall higher than a man. It had been sealed while they slept." Also add one line explaining why "north" replaces "east": "Past the cave, the old road bent north."

**S4. Mara imagines what Liv "would have said" without ever having met her** (Ch 6)
- *Where:* "'It doesn't end,' Liv would have said, Mara thought. 'It changes direction.' She had never met Liv. The thought came from the missing-name list in her notebook."
- *Problem:* A list of names cannot give her Liv's way of speaking. The passage reads like a slip.
- *Suggest:* Cut it, or give the line to someone who knows Liv: "'Liv would say it doesn't end,' Sigrid said. 'It changes direction.'"

**S5. Bjarne's rescue: the head-shake and the crying child** (Ch 10)
- *Where:* "At the third, she heard a child crying. The sound came from a broken wagon." / "Mara recognized Ingrid's description before she recognized the woman." / "'Bjarne?' Mara called. The woman shook her head. 'Ingrid?'"
- *Problem:* The child is never explained, since only Bjarne is there. Mara can't "recognize" someone she has never met. And a shake of the head reads as "No, I'm not Bjarne."
- *Suggest:* Explain the crying in one line ("The crying stopped when she reached the wagon. There was no child.") so it reads as the forest imitating voices, which Ingrid has just warned about. "Mara knew her from Ingrid's description." "Bjarne nodded, too tired to waste words. 'Ingrid?'"

**S6. "Did the road move?" "No… The roots moved it."** (Ch 10)
- *Problem:* The answer contradicts itself.
- *Suggest:* "'Not by itself.' Bjarne's eyes were tired. 'The roots moved it.'"

**S7. Noon after less than an hour of walking** (Ch 2)
- *Where:* "They set off beneath a clean blue sky. The morning…" then "At noon, Sigrid stopped. 'We haven't been walking an hour.'"
- *Problem:* The narrator states it is noon, so readers can't tell whether time has jumped or Sigrid is wrong.
- *Suggest:* "The sun already stood at noon. Sigrid stopped. 'We haven't been walking an hour.'"

**S8. Mara walks into the forest without the Portal** (Ch 13)
- *Where:* "she carried a live ember from the campfire to the forest boundary… When she reached the hollow…"
- *Problem:* Chapters 9–10 set up that the woods are reached through a short-lived Portal road ("twelve minutes"). Here she just walks out alone at dusk.
- *Suggest:* One line on the rule: "The woods nearest the clearing could be walked; it was the far places that needed the Portal's road."

**S9. Where the Heartroot fight happens** (Ch 14)
- *Where:* "The Heartroot woke when Mara brought the ember back." Then "Mara and Bjarne fought at the edge of the hollow" while "The clearing's ward faltered" and "Every root in the clearing went still."
- *Problem:* "Brought the ember back" could mean back to the hollow or back to camp. The fight seems to be in both places.
- *Suggest:* "The Heartroot followed the ember back to the clearing." Then say how close the hollow is: "The hollow lay a stone's throw past the ward."

**S10. "A child called for someone who was not there"** (Ch 17)
- *Problem:* No child lives in the clearing.
- *Suggest:* "Yrsa called a name that belonged to no one there."

**S11. Eirik judges a laugh he can no longer remember** (Ch 24; *interpretation*)
- *Where:* "One used Eirik's lost memory of his sister's laugh, rendered so perfectly that he staggered." Then "It had the same pitch, but none of the impatience."
- *Problem:* The pass took the sound of her laugh, so he cannot know whether the pitch is the same.
- *Suggest:* "One used Sigrun's laugh, the sound the pass had taken from him. Hearing it again made him stagger." He can still catch the missing impatience, because he knows her character, not the sound.

**S12. "The memory was not Mara's"** (Ch 26)
- *Where:* "Sigrid standing at the first camp… ordering everyone to eat… The memory was not Mara's."
- *Problem:* Mara was at the first camp with Sigrid (Ch 4).
- *Suggest:* "The memory was not only Mara's."

**Smaller logic gaps**
- Ch 5, "Eirik woke first": someone should have been on watch. Use "Eirik, on the last watch, saw it first."
- Ch 6, Mara puts the coal in the stone's groove with no reason given. Add half a line: "She remembered the cave fire leaning toward the stone."
- Ch 8, Ingrid knows Mara "got caught" (so does the Waker in Ch 20). Add: "I saw the light come down."
- Ch 9, Ingrid "would not let Mara look at the wound until they reached the clearing": she is already there. Use "…until she had seen the stones."
- Ch 16, "He counted the names carved there": Chapter 8 says the post has seven short lines, not names.
- Ch 27, "A stone stairway rose from the pool's depths, leading down into the mountain." It both rises and leads down. Use "As the water drew back, it uncovered a stone stairway leading down into the mountain."
- Ch 28, "Its antlers brushed the ceiling": the cavern is "large enough to hold a storm". Use "Its antlers brushed the lintel of the doors."

## 3. Needs more context

**C1. The unnamed traveler disappears** (Ch 1–6)
- *Where:* Ch 1: "a broad-shouldered traveler… Sigrid introduced them as the one who had agreed to carry the party's spare tools." Last seen in Ch 5 ("The traveler drove one back"). Not among the "seven sparks" (Ch 7).
- *Problem:* A fifth party member is never named, lost or found. "Traveler" is also used for Bjarne (Ch 10, "The missing traveler") and Yrsa (Ch 12, "The next traveler"), so readers may think those lines mean this person.
- *Why it matters:* Readers will ask where they went.
- *Suggest:* *Interpretation:* if the nameless soldier of Chapter 8 is meant to *be* the traveler (both are "they", both armed, and the soldier appears the day after the break), plant one clue, such as the soldier carrying the party's spare tools, and make the namelessness deliberate in Chapter 1: "Sigrid introduced them by a name Mara could not recall a minute later." If not, account for the traveler: lost and not heard by the Portal, or dead. Use other words for Bjarne and Yrsa ("The missing smith", "The next one to come home").

**C2. "The Waker" is used before anyone names him** (Ch 15)
- *Where:* "'Walking north.' 'After the Waker?'"
- *Problem:* No one has used the name before. Mara has only seen "a figure in a grey cloak" (Ch 13).
- *Suggest:* Let the Portal give the name: "'Walking north.' 'After what?' 'The one who wakes the doors.' Mara thought of the grey-cloaked figure. 'The man at the second stone.'" After that, "the Waker" works.

**C3. The Hunger gets no proper introduction** (Ch 15)
- *Where:* first named in passing in a summary paragraph: "held the old hunger beyond the trees… fleeing the Hunger".
- *Why it matters:* It is the main antagonist of Acts II–III.
- *Suggest:* Give it one line of dialogue: "What waits behind the doors eats names first, then the people who carried them. The old ones called it the Hunger."

**C4. The Portal gets its name with no explanation** (Ch 9)
- *Where:* "In the morning, the Portal spoke through the stones." Until then it was "the voice". Also "The portal opened." (lower case) at the end of Chapter 9.
- *Suggest:* "In the morning, the voice spoke through the stones again. Ingrid called it the Portal, as the old road-builders had." Capitalise it the same way throughout.

**C5. Game vocabulary in the prose** (Ch 9–11; *your call*)
- *Where:* "Life Orbs" (Ch 9–10), "The raid road" and "The extraction light" (Ch 10), "a place to sleep between raids" (Ch 11).
- *Problem:* In a novel, these read as game mechanics, not as part of the world.
- *Suggest:* If the book is meant as a tie-in that echoes the game, keep the terms but give each an in-world reason. Otherwise use plain words: "the narrow road", "The way home opened behind them", "between trips into the woods". "The Portal gave her twelve minutes" can become "The Portal could hold the road open only a short while".

**C6. The Portal stones the party carries in Act II, and who goes north** (Ch 19, 21, 27)
- *Where:* "The Portal had marked their map" (Ch 19). "The Portal spoke from the stones they carried" (Ch 21). "the stones at Mara's belt" (Ch 27).
- *Problem:* The Portal has been fixed in the clearing until now. It is never shown how it comes with them, who travels, or who keeps the clearing fire.
- *Suggest:* Add two lines at the end of Chapter 18 or the start of Chapter 19: "Hild chipped three small stones from the cracked arch; the Portal's voice went with them. Asa and Bread stayed behind to keep the fire." (Adjust names to your plan. Yrsa, Gunnar, Asa and Hild all appear later, so say which of them go.)

**C7. The mountain troll storyline is dropped** (Ch 6, 13, 14, 15, 23)
- *Where:* The troll mourns in Chapter 13, rings "The troll's distant bell" in Chapter 14, and is "Walking north" in Chapter 15, then disappears. Chapter 23 names the "Troll Valley" with no troll in it.
- *Suggest:* Pay it off once in Acts II–III, even briefly (the troll seen on a ridge in the valley, or standing guard outside the First Hearth), or say why the valley carries its name.

**C8. The troll line in Chapter 15 is unclear** (Ch 15; *interpretation*)
- *Where:* "The first time, the troll had saved the world badly enough to ruin a life."
- *Problem:* It does not say what "the first time" was or whose life was ruined. One possible reading is that the troll smashed the Waker's first door and shut his family on the other side. If that is the intent, it is a strong link that is currently hidden.
- *Suggest:* "Years ago it had smashed another door the same way. That had kept the Hunger out, and shut a man's wife and daughter on the other side."

**C9. What the bell is never comes clear** (Prologue, Ch 1, 14, 19, 26, 31, Epilogue)
- *Problem:* The prologue makes the bell a strong mystery ("none of them could remember the sound"). Chapter 14 gives it to the troll, Chapter 26 has name-bells, and the epilogue has a brass bell, but the book never says what a bell ringing *means*.
- *Suggest:* Use Chapter 26 to state the rule: "A bell rang when someone, somewhere, said a lost name aloud." Then the prologue, Chapter 31 and the epilogue all pay off. Drop "the troll's" in Chapter 14, or explain it.

**C10. Ingrid's blue flame and vial** (Ch 8, 9, 10, 26)
- *Where:* Ingrid carries "a blue flame" in a bottle (Ch 8), then warns "If you see a blue flame in the woods, do not touch it" (Ch 9). She gives Mara a vial that is never used (Ch 10). In Ch 26, "Ingrid poured a measure of alchemical glass into the flame" (glass can't be poured), and in Ch 30 it is "alchemical oil".
- *Suggest:* Chapter 9: one line on how her flame differs ("Mine I lit myself. The ones in the woods light themselves."). Chapter 26 pays off the vial: "Mara uncorked Ingrid's vial over the flame. It burned blue-white…". Use "oil" in both places.

**C11. Mara's mother** (Ch 1, 17, 23, 30)
- *Problem:* The mother is central to Mara's motive ("An answer", "You found the answer, then?", "the morning you left") but the book never says what happened to her: left, lost on the road, or dead. *Interpretation:* if this is held back on purpose, the ending still needs to give the bare fact, or "the truth" Mara came for has no shape.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 3, where Runa asks "Not the contract. You.", is the natural place for one or two sentences: "My mother walked this road when I was twelve. She came home, but not with her name." (Use whatever the actual story is.)

**C12. Bread's kindness happens offstage** (Ch 16)
- *Where:* "It was something they knew because Bjarne remembered it, and because the soldier had done it twice."
- *Suggest:* "Bjarne remembered a soldier who had shared his last bread with her in the woods. Since coming to the fire, he had done it twice more." Chapter 17 also says the name was "the name he had chosen for himself", but Chapter 16 ends with him unable to name himself. Add: "By noon, he had chosen one."

**C13. Sigrid's secret needs a seed in Chapter 3** (Ch 3, 27)
- *Where:* Ch 27: "I had seen a name carved into the middle stone." "'Sigrun's.' Eirik went still. 'The stone showed me a list of people…'"
- *Problems:* (a) When Eirik says "Sigrun" in Chapter 3, Sigrid does not react, and a small reaction would make the reveal land. (b) "a name" and "a list" don't match. (c) The paragraph runs Eirik's action straight into Sigrid's speech, so the line reads as Eirik's.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 3: "Sigrid looked up sharply, then back at the fire." Chapter 27: "I had seen names carved into the middle stone. One was Sigrun's." Split the paragraph: "'Sigrun's.' / Eirik went still. / 'The stone showed me a list of people who would be lost,' Sigrid said. 'I thought…'"

**C14. The ending's geography** (Ch 7, Ch 31)
- *Where:* Ch 31: "the village stood exactly where they had left it" (they never left from a village). "Eirik stood at the edge of the clearing". "Mara returned to the stone ring at the edge of the meadow". "The Waker… rebuild the houses nearest the standing stones".
- *Problem:* Readers can't tell whether the clearing, the stone ring from Chapter 6, the meadow from Chapter 1 and Granheim are one place or four. Chapter 7 also never says whether the clearing is the broken ring from Chapter 6.
- *Suggest:* Chapter 7: "It was the same ring, Mara saw, or what the ring had become." Chapter 31: name the village. Granheim, the name Liv nearly forgot in the prologue, would make a good bookend: "the old Portal stones, where a grey morning waited, and Granheim stood beyond the meadow where they had left it."

**C15. Why the Portal needs the Waker's consent** (Ch 25)
- *Suggest:* Add one clause: "…without the Waker's consent: it was his door, and he had opened it first."

**C16. The boy in Sigrun's window** (Ch 21; *interpretation*)
- *Where:* "A boy, no more than ten, sat on the table and kicked at her knee."
- *Problem:* If this is young Eirik (the window showing the past), "She's alive" means less. If it is Sigrun's son, he should appear or be mentioned in Chapter 31.
- *Suggest:* Make it clear either way, for example in Chapter 31: "Her son held her other hand."

**C17. Two blue doors** (Ch 16–17 and Ch 25; *interpretation*)
- *Where:* The soldier remembers "a kitchen with a blue door". Nessa writes "find the blue door".
- *Problem:* Readers will expect the soldier to be linked to the Waker's family.
- *Suggest:* If the link is intended, pay it off. If not, change the soldier's door to another colour.

**C18. "Guardian" means too many things** (Ch 9, 13, 14, 15, 19–31)
- *Where:* the Heartroot ("Roots. A guardian."), "the Root Guardians", "rootlings", "the forest's guardians", and the antlered "old guardian" of Act II (the cairn man calls it "him", the narration calls it "it").
- *Suggest:* Give the antlered creature its own name, such as "the Jaw-keeper", or always call it "the antlered guardian". Use "it" consistently.

**Smaller context notes**
- Prologue title "The Fourth Stone": the fourth stone is never mentioned again. Link it to the "central stone" with half a line: "the fourth stone, in the middle of the ring".
- Ch 12 title "Four Winters Out of Time": the four are only called "the four who had come from other winters" in Chapter 15. Add the phrase in Chapter 12.
- Ch 29, "the small ward fires across the forest": the first time more than one ward fire is mentioned.

## 5. Other changes

**O1. Pacing in the second half of Act I** (Ch 9, 12, 15, 18)
Chapters 9, 12 and 15 are largely summary, and Chapter 18 repeats Chapters 14–15: the broken pact, "ninety winters", "Why didn't you tell us?" asked a second time, and the road north lit a second time ("The Portal's runes lit… toward the north" at the end of Ch 14, and "a road appeared among the trees, leading north" at the end of Ch 18). Merge the new parts of Chapter 18 (the three fire-images, Mara accepting "Hearth-keeper") into Chapter 15. Use the space for a real rescue scene for Sigrid and Liv (I2).

**O2. The Portal's "I do not know" joke is used four times**
Ch 13 ("You keep saying that"), Ch 15 ("Do you ever know anything?"), Ch 27 ("Useful as ever"), Ch 28 ("You never know"). Keep the first and the last. The Chapter 28 line ("I know what it remembers. I do not know what it will choose.") is the payoff and lands harder if the joke has rested.

**O3. A repeated sentence shape: "It was not X. It was Y."**
Ch 2 ("It was not an ordinary shadow"), Ch 20 ("It was not an apology. It was the first honest thing…"), Ch 22 ("A name was not a lock. It was a hand…"), Ch 24 ("The answer was not absolution. It was…"), Ch 25 ("This was not a rescue site. It was…"), Ch 28 ("The First Hearth was not a room. It was…"), Ch 31 twice ("It was not the sound…", "It was not a wall against the dark."). Keep the last one, which is the book's closing thought, and the Chapter 22 one. Rephrase a few of the others.

**O4. "For the first time" and other "first" beats**
"For the first time" appears in Ch 11, 20, 21 and 28, and "the first honest thing" (Ch 20) and "the first answer of his that sounded like a beginning" (Ch 22) come close together. Vary two or three.

**O5. Bjarne says "Convenient" twice in a row** (Ch 18: "'Convenient,' Bjarne muttered"; Ch 19: "'Convenient,' Bjarne said."). Change one.

**O6. Two chapters in a row end and begin with the same image** (Ch 23 ends "the wall of ice sealed shut"; Ch 24 opens "The ice wall sealed behind them"). Cut the first clause of Chapter 24: "The valley began to rearrange itself."

**O7. Mara's thoughts on truth appear twice** (Ch 27: "It had not made her brave. It had only made the cost visible."; Ch 29: "It only made a choice honest."). They are close in wording and two chapters apart. Keep the Chapter 29 one, at the climax, and trim Chapter 27.

**O8. American and British spelling are mixed.** Act I uses American spelling ("color of rainwater", "cave's center", "travelers"). From Ch 19, some British forms appear: "colour" (Ch 19), "Travellers" (Ch 19), "centre" (Ch 20, 23, 28), "humourless" (Ch 24). Meanwhile "-ize" spellings continue throughout ("realized", Ch 19; "recognized", Ch 24; "organized", Ch 29; "apologize", Ch 31). Pick one style and search for those four words.

**O9. Names that look too alike: Sigrid / Sigrun / Ingrid.** Sigrid and Sigrun share a first syllable and both matter in Eirik's story ("'Whose?' 'Sigrun's.'" is spoken by Sigrid). Ingrid shares an ending with Sigrid. Consider renaming Eirik's sister to something distinct (for example Tove or Solveig).

**O10. The Portal's voice uses a contraction once.** It never uses contractions except in Ch 28: "'I don't know,' said the Portal." Use "I do not know."

**O11. "He laughed. It was not the sound he had lost in the pass."** (Ch 31). He lost the memory of *her* laugh, not his own. Suggest: "He laughed. It was not the laugh the pass had taken from him; that one had been hers. This one was new, made between them now."

**O12. A repeated exchange.** "You took your time." / "We got lost." is in Ch 26 (Sigrid and Mara) and again in Ch 31 (Sigrun and Eirik). If the echo is deliberate, it works. If not, change the Chapter 26 one so Chapter 31 keeps it.
