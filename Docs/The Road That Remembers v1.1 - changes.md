# The Road That Remembers: version 1.1 change log

- **Source:** `Docs/The Road That Remembers.docx` (left untouched; a copy was edited).
- **Review applied:** `Docs/NovelReview_TheRoadThatRemembers_2026-11-17.md`.
- **Output:** `Docs/The Road That Remembers v1.1.docx`. Same styles, fonts, page setup, header, footer and paragraph formatting as the original. The title page now reads "A dark fantasy novella · Version 1.1".
- **Length:** 13,794 words before, 14,597 after (words separated by spaces, headings included). 896 paragraphs before, 918 after. Chapter count and titles are unchanged.
- **Findings:** 91 in total (76 numbered, plus 15 in the "smaller" lists). 89 were applied with text changes. W12 kept the original line, which the review allows. O9 was not applied (see below). O1 was applied in substance but without merging chapters (see O1).
- **Bjarne:** he is a man throughout, as the author asked. Chapter 10 was the only place he was written as a woman, and it now uses he/him ("A man in travel-worn mail…", "The missing smith had a hammer at his belt", "Bjarne nodded…"). His name is unchanged.
- **Spelling:** British throughout: colour, centre, traveller(s), travelled, realised, recognised, organised, apologise. By raw word count the original leaned slightly American, but mostly because "traveler" appears 14 times. By word family it was mixed ("grey", "colour", "centre", "humourless" were already British), so I followed the British instruction. "toward" was kept, since it is also correct in British English.
