## Decisions on the review's open questions

- **Sigrun (issue 1):** I took the main version, the one that matches the game's story notes: Sigrun is one of the Lost. She went up the road years ago and never came back. The alternative (safe at home, lost later) was not used, so Ch 15 ("though they had not found her yet") stays as it is.
- **The Waker's village (issue 7, "if intended"):** yes. Granheim is the village he ran from. Everything already in the text supports it: he opened his door at "the old stones", the Portal remembers "a door opening", Granheim's name is the one being eaten in the prologue, and in Ch 31 he rebuilds "the houses nearest the standing stones".
- **Liv in Ch 26 (issue 3, optional):** applied, so her silence is explained and the scene no longer repeats Ch 12.
- **The room and the blue door (issue 8, optional clause):** applied, so the walk to the door makes sense.

## The other remaining issues

- **Issue 3, optional.** Ch 26: added a paragraph after "Good. We have that in common.": "Liv opened one eye. 'I was answering,' she told Sigrid. 'You weren't listening.'" I added "she told Sigrid" because the line now comes a few lines after Sigrid's "Answer me", and without it the reader could take it as a reply to the line just before. (Also new problem 3.)
- **Issue 4, Ch 23.** "It did not look down." → "If it saw the Waker below, it gave no sign." The review's two sentences make the troll a watcher, not a hunter, so in Ch 23 it now chooses not to act instead of simply not noticing the man it was "after". (New problem 8.)
- **Issue 5, the face in the pool (Ch 27, "if you want more").** Not applied. It is a scene cut rather than a small edit. It would also remove one of the "old bear" tests that the review lists under "What works", and "The Waker told them about the stairs" depends on it. The other repeats the review lists (the Waker's past told in pieces, the five door glimpses, the Ch 27 and Ch 30 "copy the sound" lines) had no suggested edit beyond the two cuts above, so they were left.
- **Issue 8, optional clause.** Ch 25: "The Waker watched the blue paint disappear into the rock as the passage shifted." → "…as the passage shifted, until the room was gone, and the door with it, somewhere deeper under the valley." I kept "the Waker watched" as the subject so that his next line, "That choice may be all I have left of them", still clearly belongs to him.

## Re-read after editing

I read the whole of v1.2 once, looking only for anything the edits broke. I found nothing that needed a further change. The checks that mattered:

- **Sigrun's timeline:** Eirik left home as a boy, after Sigrun stitched his thread (Ch 21: "before I left"). Later she went up the road (Ch 3). Sigrid saw her name on the stone years ago (Ch 27). Her thread lies at a camp in the snow valley (Ch 21). She comes home through the stones (Ch 31: "older than the boy she remembered"). Nothing contradicts this, and Ch 4, 12, 15 and 30 all still read correctly.
- **Pronouns in new lines:** "He nodded" (Ch 30) follows Bjarne's own line. "she told Sigrid" (Ch 26) is Liv. "Hers," he said (Ch 21) is in Eirik's paragraph.
- **The First Hearth** is now named in Ch 19 before its next mentions in Ch 23, 25, 27 and 28. Ch 28's "The old pact was made here" now pays off the Portal's line.
- **The troll and the Waker:** Ch 15 ("shut a man's wife and daughter on the other side"), Ch 23, Ch 29 and Ch 31 now tell one story.
