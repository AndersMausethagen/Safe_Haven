# Act II after the Brukam: outline for approval (2026-11-21)

The quest flow review's A21: "Act II after the Brukam: a Moss Marsh quest, the man in grey, Sigrid in Troll Valley." This is an outline to approve or change before anything is built (the rule for design documents). It follows the novel's Act II, *The Valley That Keeps* (Chapters 20–27 of *The Road That Remembers* v1.2), and keeps the game's decisions:
- the man in grey stays unnamed (decision 15);
- Sigrid is found last, in Troll Valley;
- Sigrun is one of the Lost (decision 12 and the v1.2 log);
- every quest is an event, not "gather and build";
- nothing is asked before it is taught.

## Where the game stands now

- **Built:**
  - The Road North, then The Toll, then Nobody Crosses.
  - The Brukam, now "the old one that broke the portal's stones".
  - The Moss Marsh, which opens when he falls.
- **Stubs:**
  - Runa's line ends where "the man in grey" was planned (Runa 3).
  - Sigrid's rescue quest, BringSigridHome, has its site in the Misty Woods.
  - Nothing yet uses the Moss Marsh's own story.

## For you to decide

1. **The man in grey's family.** The novel names them (Elian and Nessa). Should the game name them too, or keep them unnamed like him, as decision 15 does for him and the village?
2. **Letting him try the snow door.** Should it cost a fight at the set piece (suggested), cost something lasting (a lost memory of a companion, as the Jaw-keeper's toll did in the novel), or not be offered at all?
3. **The Jaw-keeper.** The novel has a stone-and-antler toll-keeper separate from the mountain troll. The game merged the troll with the Brukam. Should the Jaw-keeper come in, as a Tale at the Toll Troll event or in Act III, or stay out?
4. **Sigrun.** Should she stay a window in Act II and be found in Act III as one of the Lost (suggested), or be found in Act II?
5. **The Moss Marsh quest's rank.** Main (suggested, since it carries the story), or a Haven quest?
