# Menu UX research (2026-11-28)

The user (2026-11-28): "do a UX pass on the menus and tabs. Many are boring, colourless and lack good interaction and information
flow. Compare with similar menus in other games: what do they do better, what are we missing? Go through the menus systematically,
improve them, then send a UI designer to review each against the research."

Sources: Diablo IV, Enshrouded, ARC Raiders, Last Epoch, Valheim, V Rising, Tarkov, Baldur's Gate 3, Path of Exile 2, Grim Dawn,
Hades I/II, RuneScape Dragonwilds, Old School RuneScape, RimWorld, Frostpunk 2, Against the Storm, Manor Lords, Hunt: Showdown
(links in the research agent's notes below; some details are from general knowledge of the games, marked as such there).

## What all good menus share

- A short card on hover, the full detail only on select (Paradox nested tooltips: explain "without writing a novel").
- Colour that means one thing everywhere: rarity, element, slot type.
- "New" dots that clear when seen; badges on tabs that say where to look.
- Empty states that point to where to get things (locked content as silhouettes with "Learn from: …").
- A sound for each state (hover, confirm, deny); default focus and stick/D-pad navigation.
- Goals surfaced, not just numbers: "next milestone", "2 levels away", "flour runs out in 1 day".

## Per menu: top improvements for a basic version

1. **Inventory + crafting.** Upgrade/downgrade arrow on hover with a green/red compare tooltip (Diablo IV); recipe ingredients as
   icon + have/need, red when short, "×N" craftable on the card (Valheim, V Rising, Enshrouded); pin a recipe to the HUD and a "where
   to find" row for missing materials (Enshrouded, ARC Raiders); the safe pocket in its own gold frame with a lock and "kept if you
   fall" (Tarkov); "new" dots on items since the bag was last opened; rarity rims only, no badge piles.
2. **Character sheet.** 3-4 headline numbers on top (Health, Damage, Armour, Gear score), details behind (Diablo III/IV); resistances
   as coloured element chips; a stat's sources on hover ("Base 10 + Axe 6 + Meal 3"; PoE2, Grim Dawn); active effects as icons with a
   radial timer, good/bad rims (BG3); empty slots as faint silhouettes of their type, and the slot an item fits glows while it is
   held or hovered.
3. **Skill book + bar.** Each slot type its own rim colour and glyph, used on every card of that type (V Rising); clicking a slot
   filters the book to what fits, valid slots glow while dragging, a wrong drop is denied with sound (Diablo IV, Last Epoch); unknown
   skills as dark silhouettes with "Learn from: …", so an early book is a goal list, not an empty page (Hades II); "New" seal on
   freshly learned skills; hover a short card, select a detail pane.
4. **Proficiencies.** Each skill card: icon, level, progress ring, and the one next milestone as a picture ("Next: Lumber Store at
   15"; Dragonwilds, OSRS); selecting a skill shows a milestone track of pictures by level (gold earned, grey ahead); the Overview shows
   the three skills closest to a milestone; level-up toast with the icon and sound; how it is trained on hover.
5. **Haven overview.** A dashboard of picture tiles (Food, Wood, People, Mood, Defence) with big numbers, trend arrows and
   green/amber/red rims (Against the Storm, Frostpunk 2); a "Needs attention" strip whose entries jump to the cause (RimWorld
   alerts); tab badges; people as portrait cards with mood shading (RimWorld colonist bar); stores with days-left and per-day in/out on
   hover.
6. **Journal.** Quests open on one "Now" card with picture, goal and Track (BG3, Diablo IV); creatures/lore start as silhouettes with
   an unlock hint and "Seen 3/5" (Hades); "new" dots and "Mark all read"; mechanics as an illustration plus three bullets; backstory as
   a picture-led timeline with "Watch again".
7. **Pause menu.** World blurred and dimmed behind it; Resume focused; a context panel (goal, raid timer and loot so far, or day, time
   and alerts at home; Hunt: Showdown 2025); quick-jump picture buttons with hotkeys; clear save state; a raid quit spells out its cost.
8. **Results screen.** One big headline (Escaped gold / Fallen ember) over the raid's art; haul tiles animating in by rarity,
   kept before lost (lost greyed); progress as movement (bars filling, milestones bursting); on death a "what happened" card with the
   killer and one tip; one clear next step and what the haul enables (Hunt: Showdown, Tarkov, Hades).
